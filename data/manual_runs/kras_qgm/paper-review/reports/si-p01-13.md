## Content of this range

Pages 1–13 are **not** the quantum/ML part of the SI. They are §S1 *Chemistry*: general synthetic
procedures plus the step-by-step syntheses of ISM061-1, ISM061-3-2, ISM061-6, ISM061-11 and
ISM061-12, illustrated by reaction schemes (Scheme S1–S5) and ¹H NMR spectra.

Consequently there is **no code, no pseudocode, no algorithm listing, no hyperparameter table, no
circuit definition and no displayed equation anywhere in pages 1–13.** No item needed kind `code`,
`formula` or `table`, and no `si-formula-*` asset was created. There is also **no running header,
no running footer and no printed page number** anywhere in this range, so no item needed kind
`omit`; the review queue's header/footer flags all belong to the *main* document `s001-03`, not to
this supplement.

   `Pd₂(dba)₃`, `NaBH₄`, `DMSO-d₆` etc. with typographic subscripts, but the extractor emitted

## Anything I could not fully verify

Nothing in pages 1–13 was left unverified. Two honest limitations on what the verification *means*:

- The ¹H NMR spectra are preserved as images, so the peak-shift annotations, integral values and axis
  labels printed inside them are **not** searchable text and were not transcribed. That is deliberate
  (the plan forbids dumping unstructured plot labels into caption text), but it means an agent cannot
  text-search for an individual NMR peak — only for the shift lists that the surrounding prose repeats.
- The reaction schemes are likewise images; the reagent/condition annotations drawn on the arrows are
  not searchable. The prose in the Step paragraphs restates all of them.
